## What a supplier has to match

One unit at a time, printed after checkout, shipped to the buyer, with no carton of stock. Matte paper, not gloss or luster, because these are charts someone reads up close. A bright-white sheet will cool the cream field. A thin sheet or a muddy black will show in the footer type. Printful and Prodigi ask for about 300 dpi. Lumaprints’ own reply on its paper guide recommends 300 dpi minimum and JPG or PNG. The masters are 250 dpi, which is enough to sample, and the same PNG should go to every lab.
