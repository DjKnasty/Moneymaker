- The posters are unframed and ship in a tube. The spec is a poster, not a stretched canvas.

## What a supplier has to match

One unit at a time, printed after checkout, shipped to the buyer, with no carton of stock. Matte paper, not gloss, because these are charts someone reads up close. The cream field and the small footer type (about 13 pt at 18 inches) will show a thin paper or a muddy black more than a photograph would. Printful and Prodigi both ask for 300 dpi files. The masters are 250 dpi, which is enough to sample, and the same PNG should go to every lab so the comparison is fair.

Printify’s own product copy for blueprint 282 says that poster ships only to the United States and its territories. Gelato and Prodigi print in more than one country and ship beyond the US. That only matters if this page starts selling outside the US.
