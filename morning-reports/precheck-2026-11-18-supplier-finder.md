# Supplier finder — celestial art and star-map wall art — 2026-11-18

A Nov 18, 2026 read of public catalog pages prices the current unframed charts and two wall-art formats around them: Printful’s enhanced matte 18×24 poster is $13.15 plus $4.99 US shipping, and the 24×36 is $18.25 plus $7.99, on 189 g/m² paper with ISO brightness 104%; Lumaprints publishes an 18×24 stretched canvas at $28.26 to $35.69 by stretcher depth and a 24×36 archival matte sheet at $21.10, with the 18×24 paper size absent from that table; Prodigi prints giclée on 200 g/m² enhanced matte and on 310 g/m² Hahnemühle German Etching, including an 18×24 etching SKU, and publishes only from-prices (£2.50 and £3.00) plus a 50% refund on the first sample. Printify, the shop’s current poster fulfiller, still publishes Today’s Graphics US shipping at $7.39 for an 18×24 matte poster and does not publish the print price on that page. No sample was ordered, no app was installed, the shop was not opened, NightLens was not touched, and no email was sent. The three drafts below were not sent.

Prices below are published catalog readings from 18 Nov 2026. They are not a recommendation to buy, and they are not a forecast of profit.

## What this page already is

From `shopify/celestial-prints/`. This run did not open the shop.

- Four 18×24 matte posters (Ursa Major, Orion, moon phases, solar system) are the celestial line. Artwork is a 4500×6000 px PNG, 18×24 at 250 dpi, sRGB, on a warm cream field (about `#EFE6D3`). They ship unframed in a tube. Listed retail in the repo is $44.99 (18×24) and $64.99 (24×36). Fulfillment named there is Printify blueprint 282.
- Canvas, a frame, and a heavier fine-art sheet are different products from that poster. A brightened sheet cools the cream ground, so paper whiteness is part of the fit.
- WHCC, Bay Photo, Gooten, FinerWorks, Jondo, WhiteWall, and Mpix were not re-opened today.
