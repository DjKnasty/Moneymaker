## What a supplier has to match

One unit at a time, printed after checkout, shipped to the buyer, with no carton of stock. Matte paper is the match for a chart someone reads up close. A gloss or luster face mirrors the room, and a bright sheet cools the cream field.

Printful’s catalog description for the enhanced matte poster, read today, now states the sheet the marketing pages had left unnamed: **10.3 mil, 189 g/m², 94% opacity, ISO brightness 104%, paper sourced from Japan.** Brightness at 104% means optical brighteners. That is a reason to sample before treating the Printful tube as the same cream as the live Printify poster. The masters are 250 dpi sRGB. Printful and Prodigi still ask for about 300 dpi. That file is enough to sample. The same PNG should go to every lab.
