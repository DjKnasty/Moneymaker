## What a supplier has to match

One unit at a time, printed after checkout, shipped to the buyer, with no carton of stock. Matte paper, not gloss or luster, because these are charts someone reads up close. A bright-white sheet will cool the cream field. Gelato’s archival poster is the off-white sheet. Hahnemühle Photo Rag is a white cotton sheet, so a sample has to show whether `#EFE6D3` stays warm. Printful and Prodigi ask for about 300 dpi. The masters are 250 dpi, which is enough to sample, and the same PNG should go to every lab. FinerWorks borderless prints enlarge the file by about 1/10 to 1/8 inch for bleed. The spec keeps 1.2 inches of margin, so that crop stays outside the chart.
