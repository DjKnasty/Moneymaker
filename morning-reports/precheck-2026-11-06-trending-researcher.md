## Fifteen designs

### 1. Personalized night-sky star map, rectangular

A chart of the sky on a night the buyer names, with the place and a short line for a wedding, a birth, or “the night we met.”

Why it is selling: [DreamTree’s Amazon listing](https://www.amazon.com/Custom-Star-Map-Personalized-Constellation/dp/B07RC9FWLN), opened Nov 6, is $16.99 unframed, 4.8 from 7,732 ratings, “100+ bought in past month,” best-sellers rank 883 in Posters & Prints and 119,106 in Home & Kitchen. Delivery dates on that page were November 8 and November 13, 2026. Review text is anniversary, wedding, birth, and Valentine gifts. [The Night Sky](https://www.thenightsky.com/shop/star-map-print) sells the same idea at $39 on museum-grade matte and states 42,052 reviews in the care section. That count is the company, and the same page also sells jewelry, so it is not one poster’s units. [Under Lucky Stars](https://support.underluckystars.com/hc/en-us/articles/360022352254-How-to-order-what-are-the-prices) publishes US unframed prices in an article updated May 19, 2026: $130 for 12×16, $195 for 18×24, $250 for 24×36, frames about $45–$70 more, digital $29. Those figures are from the search extract of that article on this check. [Bigicraft’s customizable poster](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08PVG86J3) showed $28.99 and 4.6 from 662 reviews on a search card.

Price range seen: about $17–$39 for a basic unframed print, about $29–$60 once the sheet is 18×24 at a mid shop, and $130–$250 unframed at Under Lucky Stars. Digital files at other shops sit near $6–$29.

Target buyer: a gift buyer for a wedding, anniversary, engagement, or birth. The first photo has to be a finished example.

Risk: a static Printify poster cannot take a date and a place. Someone has to take the details, send a proof, and only then print. Star positions are facts. The layout of The Night Sky, Under Lucky Stars, or DreamTree is their design. A decorative map must not be labeled scientifically verified. [Starry Memory’s June 2026 comparison](https://starry-memory.com/custom-star-map-comparison), written by a seller in this market, says several shops run a standing “sale” off a high anchor. Read the price the buyer pays. Etsy files at a few dollars are the pressure under a $39.99 physical poster.

Already planned: the category is named as a later job in `PAINTINGS.md`. It is not one of the three paintings on the page.

The same date and place, drawn as a circle, in color, with the moon and the Milky Way, on white, black, or dark blue.

Why it is a separate design from the black-and-white rectangle: [The Stars Above Co.](https://thestarsabove.co/) says it has sold over 20,000 maps and has 2,000+ five-star reviews, and it tells buyers to skip black-and-white clip-art maps. The [Etsy shop](https://www.etsy.com/ca/shop/TheStarsAboveCo) states 12,809 sales, a 5.0 from about 1,500 reviews, and 10 years on Etsy. Latest activity on that shop page was Oct 13, 2026. Those two totals are the company’s own figures on two sites. They were not added together. The [circle print](https://thestarsabove.co/product/custom-star-map-circle/) is $35 for an 8×10 gallery print, with 12×16 at +$10, 16×20 at +$15, and 18×24 at +$25. Frames add about $51–$63. The [digital circle](https://thestarsabove.co/product/custom-star-map-circle-design-digital-download/) is $20. The Etsy Canada card for the realistic print was CA$60.02.

Price range seen: $20 digital, $35–$60 unframed depending on size, about $85–$120 once a frame is added on that site, and CA$60 on the Etsy card.

Target buyer: the same gift buyer as item 1, when they want a color night rather than a line chart. Nursery versions of this shop’s map are a side door for a new-baby gift (the shop lists them around CA$20.50).

Risk: “planetarium” and “telescopic” wording on that site is their rendering claim. This store’s rule already bars NASA and ESA imagery, so a color sky here has to be an original plot, not their file and not an agency photograph. The proof step is the same as item 1.

Already planned: no specific circular color plate. It falls under the later custom-star-map job, and it is not on the page.
