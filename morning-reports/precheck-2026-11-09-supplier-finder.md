## What a supplier has to match

One unit at a time, printed after checkout, shipped to the buyer, with no carton of stock. Matte paper is the match for a chart someone reads up close. Metal and acrylic are a second product, not a substitute for that sheet. Gelato’s aluminum help article, dated October 30, 2025, says white ink is not used, so white or transparent areas show the base: white coating on the standard panel, metallic silver on the brushed panel. A separate Gelato note describes the white finish as a satin luster and says white ink is not used on that finish either. The cream field is printed color, not bare paper, but a silver base will still take the lightest areas, and a gloss face will mirror the room. Lumaprints says the same thing about its glossy silver base: the image looks darker because the metal is the white point. Ask for a white base. Sample before any listing.

Printful and Prodigi ask for about 300 dpi. Lumaprints’ metal page asks for JPG or PNG, 300 dpi, Adobe RGB, and says 1/8 to 1/4 inch may be cropped because the metal expands in sublimation. The masters are 250 dpi sRGB. That is enough to sample. The 1.2 inch margin should keep a crop of that size off the chart. The same PNG should go to every lab.
