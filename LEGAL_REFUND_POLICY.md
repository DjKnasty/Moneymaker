# Nightlens Refund Policy

Paste this into **Shopify Admin → Settings → Policies → Refund policy** (required for checkout legal links).  
Also published on the storefront page: `/pages/shipping-returns`.

**Last updated:** November 3, 2026 (America/New_York)

---

This Refund Policy explains how Nightlens ("we," "us," or "our") handles returns, exchanges, and refunds for purchases made through our online store. By placing an order, you agree to this policy.

## 1. Eligibility

You may request a return or refund within **30 days of delivery** for most unused items purchased from Nightlens.

To qualify, items generally must be:

- Unused and in resalable condition
- In the original packaging, with all accessories, manuals, cables, and inserts included
- Free of damage not caused by a manufacturing defect or shipping mishandling

## 2. Non-returnable / final sale items

The following are generally final sale and not eligible for return or refund, except where required by law or where the item arrived defective or damaged:

- Opened software licenses, digital downloads, and activation codes
- Personalized, engraved, or custom-configured items
- Gift cards
- Items returned without original packaging or missing accessories

## 3. Defective, damaged, or incorrect items

If your order arrives defective, damaged in transit, or is not what you ordered, contact us within **30 days of delivery**. We will, at our option and at no cost to you where the issue is our fault or a carrier issue we cover:

- Repair the item
- Replace the item
- Issue a full refund of the purchase price (and original outbound shipping when we determine the issue is our fault)

Please keep all packaging and include clear photos of the damage or defect when you contact us.

## 4. How to start a return

1. Email [karonrobbkk@gmail.com](mailto:karonrobbkk@gmail.com) with your order number, the item(s) to return, and the reason for the return.
2. Wait for our written approval and return instructions before shipping anything back.
3. Ship the item using the method we specify. Do not send returns without authorization.

Unauthorized returns may be refused or delayed.

## 5. Return shipping costs

- **Our fault / defective / damaged / wrong item:** We provide a prepaid return label when approved.
- **Change of mind / other eligible returns:** Return shipping is the customer's responsibility unless we state otherwise in writing.

We recommend using a trackable method. Nightlens is not responsible for returns lost in transit without tracking.

## 6. Refunds

After we receive and inspect your return, we will notify you by email whether the return is approved.

Approved refunds are issued to the **original payment method** within **5–10 business days** after inspection. Your bank or card issuer may take additional time to post the credit.

Unless required by law or we caused the issue:

- Original shipping charges are non-refundable
- Return shipping paid by you is non-refundable

Partial refunds may apply if items are returned incomplete, used, or not in original condition.

## 7. Exchanges

We may offer an exchange for the same or an equivalent item when stock allows. If the replacement differs in price, we will collect or refund the difference before shipping the exchange.

## 8. Cancellations before shipment

If your order has not yet shipped, email us as soon as possible to request cancellation. If we successfully cancel before fulfillment, you will receive a full refund. Once shipped, the return process above applies.

## 9. Chargebacks

Please contact us before filing a payment dispute so we can help resolve the issue. Unresolved chargebacks may delay or limit future purchases.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision. Changes apply to orders placed after the updated policy is posted.

## 11. Contact

Questions about returns or refunds:  
[karonrobbkk@gmail.com](mailto:karonrobbkk@gmail.com)  
Nightlens Online Store
