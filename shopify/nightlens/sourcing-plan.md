# Nightlens: Telescope Sourcing Plan Under $2,000

## Summary

Nightlens (duv5f8-wt.myshopify.com) is a US Shopify store that resells smart telescopes and accessories from established brands under those brands' own names, as an authorized reseller. First inventory budget is under $2,000 total, including samples, shipping, and duty.

## Recommendation

DwarfLab is the only brand on the list with both a public dealer application form and an existing email thread with the owner. Apply there first. Commit $637 today to one DWARF 3 plus two attach items, purely as an evaluation and content asset, and hold the remaining $1,363 until DwarfLab names a dealer discount of 17% or better.

## Supplier contacts and what to ask each

1. **DwarfLab**, hello@dwarflab.com, plus the form at dwarflab.com/us/pages/reseller. Ask for dealer discount off US retail for DWARF mini, DWARF 3, and Draco; minimum opening order; MAP policy in writing; and confirmation Nightlens will be listed on the Find Authorized Reseller page. The listing matters most because DwarfLab's warranty page ties coverage to purchase from an authorized store. Ask for 25% and a $1,300 opening order.

2. **Explore Scientific**, orders@explorescientific.com, 1-866-252-3811, kent@explorescientific.com for specialty dealers. They are a DwarfLab authorized reseller and a Celestron Premier Select dealer, and they run a wholesale storefront on Faire with a $100 minimum, free first-order returns, and Net 60 if approved. Ask whether they will open a wholesale account for an online-only US retailer, what the minimum is, whether they drop ship, and for a DWARF quote.

3. **Woodland Hills Camera and Telescopes**, quote@telescopes.net, 818-347-2270. One question: do you wholesale to online-only resellers, and at what minimum. They hold both Celestron Premier Select and DwarfLab authorization, so they are a route to product without either brand evaluating a new store.

## What could not be verified

- Every dealer discount and every minimum opening order on all five brands. None are published. All quote required.
- All MAP policies. No brand publishes one, which is not the same as none existing.
- Whether Explore Scientific will wholesale DWARF 3 or DWARF mini to a new store. Their Faire storefront shows only the discontinued DWARF II Solar Elite at a $472 MSRP and a $100 minimum.
- ZWO and Celestron warranty term lengths from a primary page.
- The 12.5% forced-labor duty figure in the import section came from secondary tariff sources and needs a customs broker to confirm.

## Useful negative findings

- OPT Telescopes closed in 2023 and its assets went to Agena Astro, so any OPT dealer program page is dead.
- Agena Astro, High Point Scientific, and Astronomics publish no wholesale program at all, so they are competitors rather than suppliers.
- No brand on the list offers dropshipping. DwarfLab's real zero-inventory option is a 5% affiliate program, about $27 on a $549 scope, which is the honest starting point if traffic is unproven.
