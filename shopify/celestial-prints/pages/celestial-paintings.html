Star maps and celestial charts, printed when you order them. These are unframed paintings on matte fine-art paper. No copy is kept on a shelf. A frame shown in a room photo is not part of the order.

Each sheet is one subject, set like an old atlas page: warm paper, engraved line, and a named source. They are not a custom night sky for a date or a city.

The four paintings are in the Celestial Paintings collection.
