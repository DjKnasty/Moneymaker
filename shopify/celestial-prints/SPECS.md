## Print notes

- Canvas 4500 × 6000 px = 18 × 24 in at 250 dpi. Safe for 18 × 24 and any smaller 3:4 size.
- Margin: 300 px (1.2 in) to the outer keyline, 340 px to the inner rule — no artwork inside the trim margin.
- Colour: RGB, sRGB intent. Convert to the printer's CMYK profile at upload if the POD vendor requires it.
- Background is a flat warm cream field (≈ #EFE6D3) with subtle paper grain, not a photograph of paper, so it prints cleanly without banding.
- All type is real vector-quality rendering at 250 dpi; smallest footer text is ~46 px cap height (≈ 13 pt at 18 in width).

## Rights summary

Every source plate is a pre-1931 publication and marked Public domain on Wikimedia Commons
(PD-old-100-expired, PD-Art, PD-old-70-1923). No NASA or ESA imagery is used anywhere.
Full metadata, direct upload URLs, and dimensions are in `sources.json`.
